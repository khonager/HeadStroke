HeadStroke privacy

HeadStroke processes head-motion samples from your Even G2 to position a drawing cursor. Live samples are used in memory and are not uploaded. Calibration values, sensitivity, your current canvas and saved drawings are stored locally through the Even app.

HeadStroke does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, external servers, location, microphone, camera or photo-library access. It requests no additional platform permissions. The Even Realities app and platform operate under their own privacy policy.

You can delete saved drawings in the sketchbook. Clearing the Even app's data or uninstalling may erase your drawings and settings. There is no cloud backup. Browser preview uses separate local browser storage.

Questions: open an issue at HeadStroke on GitHub.
